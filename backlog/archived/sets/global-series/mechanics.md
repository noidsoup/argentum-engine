# Global Series: Jiang Yanggu & Mu Yanling (GS1) — Mechanics

Standard keyword mechanics only — all supported by existing SDK primitives at set completion.

---

## Keyword mechanics

### - [x] Flying (7 cards)

Creatures with flying can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach (CR 702.9).

**Engine support:** `Keyword.FLYING`

Cards: Ancestor Dragon, Colorful Feiyi Sparrow, Fire-Omen Crane, Heavenly Qilin, Screeching Phoenix, Stormcloud Spirit, Welkin Tern

### - [x] Enchant (2 cards)

Aura spells target a permanent or player and attach on resolution (CR 303.4).

**Engine support:** Aura attachment via `Patterns` / standard enchantment typing

Cards: Cloak of Mists, Hardened-Scale Armor

### - [x] Reach (2 cards)

Creatures with reach can block creatures with flying (CR 702.17).

**Engine support:** `Keyword.REACH`

Cards: Feiyi Snake, Giant Spider

### - [x] Trample (1 card)

Excess combat damage from a trampling attacker can be dealt to the defending player or planeswalker (CR 702.19).

**Engine support:** `Keyword.TRAMPLE`

Cards: Earthshaking Si

## Set themes

Planeswalker-focused duel deck (Jiang Yanggu & Mu Yanling), Chinese-mythic creatures, and a pair of
multi-zone tutor sorceries (Journey for the Elixir, Rhythmic Water Vortex). No set-specific keyword
mechanic.
